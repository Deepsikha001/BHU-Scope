# BHU-Scope
BHU-SCOPE AI: Beneath Horizon Understanding for Sustainable Characterization, Optimization, Prospecting &amp; Exploration. A Physics-Aware Decision Intelligence multimodal AI platform that integrates seismic, geological, geospatial, and environmental intelligence to recommend the safest, most sustainable, and economically optimal drilling locations.

###Industry Problem:
Oil and gas exploration remains expensive, uncertain, and environmentally sensitive. Exploration teams rely on multiple disconnected software tools for seismic interpretation, geological analysis, hazard assessment, reserve estimation, and environmental evaluation. This fragmentation slows decision-making and increases the risk of drilling non-productive or hazardous wells.
Current workflows often answer "Where are hydrocarbons?" but do not simultaneously answer "Should we drill here?"

###Proposed Solution:
Develop a physics-aware AI decision intelligence platform capable of:
identifying promising hydrocarbon reservoirs,
assessing geological hazards,
estimating recoverable energy,
evaluating environmental impact,
ranking drilling sites using multi-objective optimization,
providing explainable recommendations through an interactive digital twin dashboard.
